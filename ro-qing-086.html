Famille Rose Sgraffito Double-Gourd Vase on Celadon Ground

Double-gourd (hulu) form vase with a pale celadon-green ground incised in fine sgraffito scrollwork; overglaze famille rose peonies, lotus, and hibiscus with a bold iron-red lattice motif; a scalloped lotus-petal border in pink and yellow near the foot; turquoise-glazed recessed base with an iron-red seal-form mark.

Double-Gourd (Hulu) Form Celadon Sgraffito Ground Famille Rose Florals Iron-Red Seal Mark Qing Dynasty

Object Description

The vase has a double-gourd (hulu) silhouette: a tall, gently waisted neck flaring to a scalloped, multicoloured lip, an upper small bulb, and a larger lower globular body, all set on a spreading foot. A raised, gilt medallion enclosing the character shou (壽, "longevity") in a circular seal-script form sits at the waist between the upper and lower bulbs, confirming the longevity motif noted in the accompanying documentation.

The body is covered overall in a pale celadon-green ground, finely incised with a dense scrolling sgraffito pattern beneath the glaze. Painted over this ground in famille rose enamels are peonies, lotus buds, and a many-petalled bloom resembling a chrysanthemum, in soft pinks, yellows, and blues with green foliage. A bold, thick iron-red band in an angular, fretwork/lattice-like pattern wraps around the lower body.

Near the foot, a scalloped, lotus-petal-form border is painted in graduated pink and yellow tones with a fine red wave pattern within each petal, above a narrow iron-red line. The base is glazed turquoise and set within a slightly recessed foot; at its centre is a square seal-form mark in iron-red enamel, arranged as a compact grid of character-units.

Note
The accompanying documentation reads this as a six-character iron-red seal mark, 大清乾隆年製 (Da Qing Qianlong nian zhi). The mark's division into a compact grid is broadly consistent with a six-character arrangement, though individual strokes are not all fully resolved at the resolution supplied. The mark is recorded here per the accompanying documentation, and — as with any mark — it is considered together with the vessel's form and decoration rather than as an isolated proof of date.

The accompanying documentation describes four such shou medallions distributed around the upper bulb. The photograph supplied shows a single view of the vase, in which one medallion is visible; the remaining three could not be checked, as no photographs from other angles were supplied.

Optical Microscopy

The observations below concern a red/white/blue enamel boundary and a purple enamel area, examined by optical/macro photography. This section is limited to optical features; the elemental composition of the pigments and glaze is not addressed here.

Enamel Boundary Detail

Instrument Unconfirmed

A narrow band of deep red enamel with a granular texture runs alongside a paler, glossy white zone, which in turn meets a pale blue-grey area with a scattering of small dark specks and fine hairline scratches. The transitions between the three zones are fairly distinct. The imaging device and exact magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary.

Purple Enamel Area

Instrument Unconfirmed

A lavender-purple enamel surface shows a fine, even granular texture with scattered small dark specks and a few faint hairline marks. A darker, coarser-textured zone is visible at the upper left of the frame. The imaging device and magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary and limited to optical appearance.
