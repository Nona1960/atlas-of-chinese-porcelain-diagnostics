Yellow-Ground Famille Rose Dish with Floral Scrolls

Flat circular dish with a gently raised rim; brilliant yellow enamel ground painted overall with famille rose peonies, lotus blossoms, and other flowers amid scrolling foliage; a continuous vegetal scroll border; white-glazed reverse with a partially visible overglaze red seal-form mark.

Yellow-Ground Famille Rose Floral Scroll Border Overglaze Red Seal Mark Qing Dynasty

Object Description

The dish is flat and circular with a gently raised rim. The interior is covered overall in a bright yellow enamel ground, densely painted in famille rose enamels with large peony blossoms, lotus-like flowers, smaller star-shaped blooms, and scrolling green foliage in a continuous, all-over composition without a reserved central medallion. The palette includes pink, blue, white, pale green, and touches of iron-red.

The exterior/underside rim carries a matching yellow-ground floral scroll border, with similar peonies, small blue flowers, and scrolling foliage, continuing the decorative scheme from the interior.

A fragment of an overglaze red mark is visible at the edge of the frame in the supplied rim photograph, showing part of at least one character in red enamel on the white-glazed reverse.

Note
The accompanying documentation describes this as a "six-character overglaze red seal-script reign mark 大清光緒年製" (Guangxu period). No photograph showing the complete mark was supplied — only a small fragment is visible at the corner of one image. The full text, character count, and script style of the mark could not be independently checked against a photograph; the reading is recorded here only as the documentation's own claim.

No measurement scale appears in the photographs; the documentation's stated diameter of approximately 27 cm is noted but not independently verified.

Optical Microscopy

The observation below concerns a pale glaze/ground area, examined by optical/macro photography. This section is limited to optical features; the elemental composition of the glaze and pigments, and any assessment of age or firing history, is not addressed here.

Pale Glaze/Ground Area

Instrument Unconfirmed

A pale cream-toned surface shows scattered fine dark specks of varying size, and a curved painted or incised outline crosses the lower part of the frame. The surface reads closer to a pale cream/ivory tone in this image than to the "brilliant yellow" described elsewhere for the ground, though this may reflect the specific area photographed or the lighting used. The imaging device and exact magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary and limited to optical appearance.
