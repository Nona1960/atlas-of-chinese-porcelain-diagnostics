Miniature Clair-de-Lune Vase with Famille Rose Blossoming Branch and Poetic Inscription

大清乾隆年制 — Dà Qīng Qiánlóng Nián Zhì · Clair-de-lune Glazed Chamber Vase with Fencai Enamels and Calligraphic Inscription, Qianlong Mark and Period (1736–1795)

Clair-de-Lune (Yueh Pai) Glaze Qing Dynasty · Qianlong 1736–1795 Jingdezhen Kilns (attributed) Famille Rose Branch-and-Bird Motif Underglaze-Blue Seal Mark Optical Microscopy Observations

Object Description

A small baluster-form chamber vase with a flared neck and everted rim, covered in an even, pale lavender-toned monochrome glaze (clair de lune / yueh pai), a decorative type associated with Qing imperial taste.

Inscribed side, and the base bearing the underglaze mark.

The painting is built up in layered, semi-transparent enamels with soft tonal gradation: the blossom petals are rendered in a gentle pink-white modelling, the branch in ochre-brown tones.

Macro photography of the painting and inscription (ordinary camera, not microscope) — details are visible.

The inscription is executed in black enamel with visible uneven thickening of the brush strokes at their beginnings and ends and slight enamel bleed on the glaze surface.

The base, beneath a clear glaze, carries a six-character mark reading "Da Qing Qianlong Nian Zhi" (大清乾隆年制) in seal script (zhuanshu).

Optical Microscopy

The observations below concern the glaze of the vessel, examined by optical gemmological microscopy — bubble morphology, the character of layer boundaries, and enamel structure.

Glaze Bubble Structure

Optical Microscopy

Numerous bubbles of varying diameter are visible within the glaze body, unevenly distributed.

Enamel / Glaze Interface Near the Seal

Instrument Unconfirmed

At the edge of the red seal enamel a grainy layer of pigment of uneven density is visible.

Painted Enamel Surface (Branch)

Instrument Unconfirmed

The surface of the dark branch enamel shows a fine-grained, slightly uneven texture.

This section is limited to optical observations of the glaze and enamels (bubble morphology, boundary character, surface grain).
