Gilt-Relief Ground Vase with Famille Rose Landscape

Tall vase with waisted neck and broad body; neck and shoulder decorated with an embossed gilt-relief ground bearing raised roundels and a stylised lotus-lappet band, alternating with white cartouches painted with pink and purple flowers; the main body with a famille rose shanshui (mountains-and-water) landscape in blue-green and ochre tones; turquoise-glazed base bearing a raised gilt seal-form mark.

Gilt-Relief Ground Famille Rose Landscape Shanshui Painting Turquoise Base Gilt Seal Mark Qing Dynasty

Object Description

The vase has a tall, waisted neck rising to a narrow, slightly flared rim, and a broad ovoid body set on a short foot. The neck and shoulder carry an embossed gilt-relief ground: raised roundels enclosing stylised, densely worked motifs are set against a textured gilt field, bordered below by a band of stylised lotus lappets in low relief. Alternating with the roundels are white-reserved cartouches painted in famille rose enamels with sprays of pink and purple blossoms.

The main body is painted with a shanshui (mountains-and-water) landscape: layered blue-green peaks rendered with soft tonal gradation suggest mist and distance, with pine trees, a thatched pavilion, a fenced enclosure, and small figures in an idealised riverside setting, all in a restrained palette of blue-green, ochre, and soft pink accents. Narrow gilt bands frame the rim and the foot.

The base is glazed turquoise and bears a raised, gilt, square seal-form mark with a dense linear/geometric pattern.

Note
At the resolution supplied, the individual characters of this mark are not clearly legible as readable script; it presents as a compact grid of raised gilt lines rather than a clearly identifiable seal-text reading. The accompanying documentation titles the piece "Imperial Kilns, Qianlong Period (1736–1795)" but does not supply a specific reading of this mark. Without a legible reign mark, this period and kiln attribution cannot be independently confirmed from the photographs and is recorded here only as the documentation's stated framing, not as a verified fact.
Note
The accompanying documentation states that "the interior is covered with a turquoise glaze." The supplied photographs show the exterior base disc glazed turquoise; no photograph of the vessel's mouth or neck interior was supplied, so the interior-glaze claim could not be independently checked against an image and is noted here as stated in the documentation rather than confirmed.

Optical Microscopy

The observations below concern a purple/pink floral enamel area, a blue-toned area, and the gilt surface, examined by optical/macro photography. This section is limited to optical features; the elemental composition of the pigments and glazes is not addressed here.

Purple/Pink Floral Enamel

Instrument Unconfirmed

A magenta-purple enamel surface shows a fine, even granular texture crossed by a network of dark, irregular crackle lines, with scattered small bright specular points along some of the cracks. The imaging device and exact magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary and limited to optical appearance.

Blue-Toned Area

Instrument Unconfirmed

The image is soft and low-contrast, showing a mottled blue-grey and white surface without a clearly resolved texture or boundary. Given the blur, only a general colour impression can be reported here rather than a specific microstructural observation. The imaging device and magnification are not documented.

Gilt Surface Texture

Instrument Unconfirmed

The gilt area shows a fine, directional, granular texture with fine linear scratches and alternating bands of brighter and duller tone, consistent with a worn or unevenly reflective metallic surface. The imaging device and magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary and limited to optical appearance.
