Catalogue No. RO-QING-090

Cobalt-Blue Ground Vase with Famille Rose Figural Medallion

Monumental vase of cylindrical form with a tall flaring neck; deep cobalt-blue ground overpainted with fine gilt floral scrollwork; a principal famille rose medallion depicting a genre scene with figures in a landscape, together with subsidiary marbleized pink roundels; turquoise-glazed base with a small underglaze-blue seal-form mark.

Cobalt-Blue Ground Gilt Scrollwork Famille Rose Figural Medallion Marbleized Pink Roundels Qing Dynasty

Object Description

The vase has a large, cylindrical body rising to a tall, flaring neck with an everted lip, standing on a glazed foot. The exterior is covered overall in a deep, even cobalt-blue ground, overpainted with fine continuous gilt scrollwork of stylised flowers and foliage. A band of pink heart-shaped lappets and a diaper border separate the neck from the shoulder, and a further diamond-lattice band appears at the foot.

The principal decoration is a large famille rose medallion showing a genre scene: a woman with upswept hair, wearing a pink garment with a yellow collar, seated beside two children, in a landscape with a gnarled pine tree, distant mountains, and water. The figures' faces, hair, and dress are painted in a manner closely resembling European genre painting rather than traditional Chinese figural conventions, consistent with the accompanying documentation's description of European pictorial influence.

Subsidiary roundels elsewhere on the body show a marbleized pink and white pattern imitating veined stone, bordered in gilt.

The base is glazed turquoise and bears a small, square seal-form mark in underglaze blue.

Note
The accompanying documentation describes this as a "six-character Qianlong reign mark... within a double circle." In the supplied photograph, the mark clearly shows four character-units (乾隆年製 in the arrangement visible) arranged in a 2×2 grid within a single square border — not six characters, and not a double circle. This is recorded as an open discrepancy between the documentation and the photograph; the mark is described here as seen (a four-character, square-bordered underglaze-blue mark), and — as with any mark — it is considered together with the vessel's form and decoration rather than as an isolated proof of date.

Optical Microscopy

The observation below concerns the marbleized pink enamel medallion, examined by optical/macro photography. This section is limited to optical features; the elemental composition of the pigment and glaze is not addressed here.

Marbleized Pink Enamel

Instrument Unconfirmed

A rounded field of mauve-pink enamel with a fine, mottled, vein-like internal pattern is bordered by a granular gilt band and a dark outline. The mottling within the pink field shows soft, irregular light and dark patches rather than sharp lines. The imaging device and exact magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary and limited to optical appearance.
