Yellow-Ground Famille Rose Vase with Yongzheng Reign Mark

雍正年製黄地粉彩瓶 — Yōngzhèng Nián Zhì Huángdì Fěncǎi Píng

Famille Rose · Yangcai 洋彩 / Fencai 粉彩 Qing Dynasty · Yongzheng 1723–1735 Jingdezhen Kilns Yellow Ground + Turquoise Interior Mark: 雍正年製 Dronova GOM / BEINOS Method

Object Description

The vase represents the classical imperial form Yan Yan ("blossoming beauty"): a globular body with a tall cylindrical neck and a wide flared mouth, characteristic of the Yongzheng period. The form reflects the refined aesthetic of the imperial kilns at Jingdezhen.

The exterior surface is covered with a saturated lemon-yellow enamel ground with an engraved scroll pattern incised into the biscuit beneath the glaze. This technique demonstrates the sophisticated craftsmanship of the Yongzheng atelier, combining mechanical sgraffito decoration with polychrome famille rose overglaze painting.

Additional details of the decoration are visible in the overall view (Fig. 1), showing the complete composition of the famille rose palette.

Chemical and Technological Interpretation

The saturated lemon-yellow ground, according to published research, was created using a lead-antimony-tin enamel (Naples Yellow type), which produces the characteristic high transparency and even surface finish observed on this piece. The color does not exhibit the matte or opaque appearance of later imitations.

Beneath the ground enamel, a relief ornament of scrollwork is clearly visible—the pattern was physically incised into the biscuit before glazing. This mechanical sgraffito technique is a key diagnostic marker of authentic Yongzheng imperial production, requiring skilled hand-engraving of the unfired porcelain body.

The combination of the yellow external ground with turquoise interior glaze on the neck and base is a characteristic feature of Yongzheng-period monochrome and polychrome wares, reflecting the palette preferences of the imperial court at that time.

Mark clarification: In the preliminary description, the mark was characterized as "applied in blue underglaze." However, close examination of high-resolution photography reveals that the mark reads as an impressed relief mark, with the characters formed in the same tone as the surrounding glaze. This is consistent with the technique of carving the mark into the biscuit before final firing.

Diagnostic Checklist

  • Imperial Yan Yan form: globular body, tall cylindrical neck, wide flared mouth, low ring foot
  • Lemon-yellow ground enamel, even and highly transparent, consistent with a Naples-Yellow-type (Pb–Sn–Sb) pigment
  • Background scroll pattern physically incised into the biscuit beneath the yellow enamel (mechanical sgraffito)
  • Polychrome famille rose overglaze decoration — peony, orchid/lily, fruit cluster, butterfly
  • Uniform turquoise glaze inside the neck and covering the base
  • Reign mark 雍正年製 in a square cartouche, reading as an impressed relief mark under the glaze
  • Homogeneous overglaze enamel structure without visible retouching or later overpainting
  • No elemental (XRF/Raman) data yet obtained for this object — full confirmation pending
The combination of diagnostic features — the Yan Yan form, the engraved background pattern beneath the yellow enamel, the polychrome famille rose decoration with careful tonal modeling, the impressed reign mark, and the turquoise interior glaze — together constitute a coherent and internally consistent profile of an imperial Yongzheng production. This vase meets the criteria of the Dronova GOM/BEINOS method for high-confidence attribution to the Yongzheng period (1723–1735) and the Jingdezhen imperial kilns.
