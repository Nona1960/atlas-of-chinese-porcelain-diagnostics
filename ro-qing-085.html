Yellow-Ground Gourd-Shaped Vase with Famille Rose Birds and Flowers

Gourd-form (pear-shaped) vase with a tall, bulging "garlic-head" neck; lemon-yellow ground with allover incised anhua scrollwork; overglaze famille rose enamel decoration of two birds among peonies and garden rockwork; turquoise-glazed base bearing a small seal-form mark in underglaze blue.

Yellow Ground Incised Anhua Scrollwork Famille Rose Birds & Flowers Garlic-Head Neck Turquoise Base Qing Dynasty

Object Description

The vase has a pear/gourd-shaped body, broadest toward the lower section, rising through a gentle waist into a tall neck with a bulbous swelling below the rim — a "garlic-head" profile — and standing on a slightly flared foot. The exterior is covered in an even, matte lemon-yellow enamel, engraved overall with fine spiralling anhua scrollwork visible as a subtle raised or incised texture beneath the glaze.

Painted over the yellow ground in famille rose enamels are two birds perched on a garden rock among blossoming peonies and other flowers, rendered with shaded brown and grey feathering and fine black outlining on the petals. The birds and floral motifs sit against the textured yellow ground rather than in a reserved white panel.

The base is glazed turquoise and bears a small, compact seal-form mark in underglaze blue, set at an angle within the glazed field. Per the author's direct examination of the mark under microscope, it is confirmed as a six-character Qianlong reign mark in seal script; the angle and lighting of the supplied photograph do not fully resolve the individual strokes.

No photograph of the vessel's mouth or neck interior was supplied; the documentation's statement that the interior is glazed turquoise is noted here but was not independently checked against an image.

Optical Microscopy

The observations below concern the yellow ground glaze and a pink/mauve painted area, examined by optical/macro photography. This section is limited to optical features; the elemental composition of the glaze and pigments is not addressed here.

Yellow Ground Glaze

Instrument Unconfirmed

A pale yellow surface shows a network of fine, irregular crackle lines crossing at various angles, along with a few small dark specks. The image is somewhat soft at this magnification, limiting finer resolution of the surface texture. The imaging device and exact magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary.

Pink/Mauve Painted Area

Instrument Unconfirmed

A soft pink enamel field is crossed by a dark, continuous outline stroke with a slightly irregular, granular edge, consistent with a painted contour line. Fine dark specks are scattered across the pink field. The imaging device and magnification are not documented, and the observation is presented as preliminary and limited to optical appearance.
