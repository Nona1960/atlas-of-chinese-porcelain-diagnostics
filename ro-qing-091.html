Catalogue No. RO-QING-091

Vase with Grasshopper and Flowering Branch, Shen De Tang Pavilion Mark

Cylindrical vase with a gently flaring neck; wine-red monochrome glaze at the neck/mouth and foot framing a central white pictorial panel; overglaze painting of a grasshopper and other insects on a flowering branch with red blossoms; base bearing the pavilion mark 慎德堂製 (Shen De Tang zhi) in iron-red within a square border.

Wine-Red Monochrome Zones Grasshopper & Flowering Branch Shen De Tang Pavilion Mark Qing Dynasty, Daoguang Period

Object Description

The vase has a cylindrical body with a gently flaring neck and mouth, standing on a plain foot. The neck/mouth and the lower body/foot are covered in a deep, even wine-red monochrome glaze, framing a central white panel that runs around the body.

Within the white panel, a flowering branch with clusters of small red/pink blossoms, blue-green leaves, and a pale yellow daisy-like flower is rendered in fine overglaze brushwork with black contour lines. A grasshopper and a second, smaller flying insect are painted on and near the branch. The style is fine and naturalistic, with careful attention to the structure of the leaves and stems.

The accompanying documentation describes a calligraphic poetic inscription in black ink on the reverse of the vase.

Note
No photograph of the reverse side was supplied, so the inscription could not be checked against an image. This entry does not describe the calligraphy beyond noting that it is claimed by the documentation.

The base is glazed white and bears a square, iron-red seal-form mark reading 慎德堂製 (Shen De Tang zhi, "Made for the Hall of Prudent/Reflective Virtue"), a pavilion-style mark rather than a reign-year mark. The four characters are clearly legible in the supplied photograph and match the accompanying documentation's reading.
